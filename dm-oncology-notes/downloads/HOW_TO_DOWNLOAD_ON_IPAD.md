# How to download on iPad (Safari)

Open these links **in Safari** (not inside the Cursor chat viewer).

## Step 1 — Open one PDF first (no unzip needed)

1. Tap this link:
   https://github.com/drmaitreyi99-cyber/Maitu-oncoatlas-trial/blob/cursor/dm-oncology-exam-notes-e22b/dm-oncology-notes/downloads/NCCN_2026_Updates_CheatSheet.pdf
2. Tap the **Download raw file** / download icon (top right).
3. Choose **Save to Files** → On My iPad / iCloud Drive.
4. Open the **Files** app → tap the PDF.

## Step 2 — Best exam pack (72 PDFs)

1. Open:
   https://github.com/drmaitreyi99-cyber/Maitu-oncoatlas-trial/blob/cursor/dm-oncology-exam-notes-e22b/dm-oncology-notes/downloads/DM_Oncology_Focus_2022_2026.zip
2. Tap **Download raw file**.
3. Save to **Files**.
4. In Files, tap the zip → it will unzip → open any PDF.
