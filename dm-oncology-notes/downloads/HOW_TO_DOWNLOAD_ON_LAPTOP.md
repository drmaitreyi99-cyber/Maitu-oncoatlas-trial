## What happens after you click

- Chrome/Edge: file appears in the bottom download bar → click the arrow → **Show in folder**
- If a PDF opens in a new tab: press **Ctrl+S** (Windows) or **Cmd+S** (Mac) → Save
- For ZIP: double-click → Extract → open the PDF files inside
